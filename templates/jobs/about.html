About

A practical job search workspace.

FindYourJob brings the bot's vacancy collection and matching workflow to the web, with your profile, saved jobs, history, and a small amount of AI assistance in one place.

Search and recommendations are handled by Python and PostgreSQL. AI is limited to CV generation and vacancy analysis, with a deterministic fallback when no API key is configured.
